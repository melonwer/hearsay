01 · Honest ranges & wording tests

Rewording a buyer question swings AI answers far more than repeating one prompt (arXiv:2605.27440). Hearsay tests multiple buyer wordings and shows Wilson 95% confidence ranges instead of fake single-run precision.

02 · Each AI assistant kept separate

ChatGPT Search, Claude Search, Gemini Search, and coding agents are tracked individually—never blended into a mystery score. Timed-out searches are excluded instead of counted as brand misses.

03 · Skipped pages tied to real leads

Hearsay separates pages AI opened from pages AI actually cited, so you know what to fix—and compares updated pages against untouched control pages to measure net lead lift honestly.
